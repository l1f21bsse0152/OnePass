# OnePass Feature Highlights

## Traveler experience

- Browse facilities and view facility details.
- View pass and wallet information.
- Scan facility QR codes to request access.
- Review monthly wallet and facility activity.
- Manage account and profile settings.

## Facility and administrator experience

- Facility accounts can view check-in history and generate facility QR codes.
- Administrators have dashboard views for platform activity, users, facilities, transactions, and access activity.
- Dashboard sample data is served separately from live records and is not inserted into MongoDB.

## QR access

Facility QR codes support the access flow between a traveler and a facility. The dashboard documentation notes that QR tokens are stored as hashes and are returned only once when generated.
